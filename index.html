Patient Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear answers to common queries regarding root canals, dental implants, teeth cleaning & checkups at Dr. Aayas Dental Clinic.

Is a Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful?

▼
Not at all! With modern rotary endodontics and computerized local anesthesia used by Dr. Sumeet Aya, root canal treatment is completely pain-free and often completed in a single comfortable sitting.

How long do Dental Implants last?

▼
Dental implants are made from biocompatible pure titanium that fuses permanently with your jawbone (osseointegration). With routine brushing, flossing, and 6-month checkups, dental implants can easily last a lifetime.

Does Teeth Cleaning (Scaling) weaken or loosen teeth?

▼
This is a common myth. Ultrasonic scaling only vibrates off hardened tartar and harmful bacterial plaque that would otherwise destroy bone and loosen teeth. Cleaning protects your gums and keeps teeth tightly anchored!

What are the clinic timings and how can I book?

What safety & sterilization standards do you follow?

▼
We follow strict hospital-grade sterilization with Class-B vacuum autoclaving, individual sealed sterile pouch packaging for all handpieces, and 100% disposable barrier protection for every single patient.
